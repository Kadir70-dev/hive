Why we exist

Abu Dhabi has always known the art of gathering.

Inspired by the warmth of the majlis, we bring people together through meaningful experiences, creativity, heritage and care for the land.

The Hive

A community, not just events

So we’re bringing the majlis back.The Hive Society brings people of all nationalities together in Abu Dhabi. Discover thoughtfully curated gatherings for everyone, with dedicated experiences for women.

Belong

Small gatherings, genuine conversations and a warm welcome for everyone.

Create

Pottery, palm weaving, writing, photography. A space to try new things and share what we make.

Care for the land

Every gathering is hosted sustainably, and every season we plant something for Abu Dhabi.

"Like the ghaf, we grow slowly, with deep roots, so we last."
Who it's for

For Abu Dhabi, for everyone

Men and women of all nationalities are welcome. Join for creative experiences, cultural heritage, sustainability and meaningful connections.

A note from our founder

A community that isn't sustainable won't last — and a city cannot flourish without a strong, creative community behind it.

I started The Hive because I believe the best of Abu Dhabi is built by people who truly know one another. Nothing worth keeping grows in a day. Like the ghaf, we plant slowly and deeply — one gathering, one friendship, one tree at a time.

Meera Al AhbabiFounder, The Hive Society
